Our Mission: Guilford University will provide students with an educational opportunity to become continuous learners who are proficient, responsible and productive citizens.

Guilford University was opened in 1965 as a womens only school. That changed in 1986 making it one of the biggest public universities in North Carolina thanks to the increased band of students being admitted. In 2004, the school was renovated adding a new building, housing administrative and guidance offices, six science labs, an auditorium and several new classrooms. The media center was also expanded to include two computer labs, and the former auditorium was reconfigured to contain performing arts classrooms for band, chorus, dance, and orchestra. Today, Guilford University is home to around 15,000 students of diverse backgrounds who excel academically, artistically, and athletically.
