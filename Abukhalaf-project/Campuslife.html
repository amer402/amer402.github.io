Student Life

The Student Life Division at Guilford University seeks to provide a journey for students to engage in experiences that broaden the mind, strengthen the body and inspire the spirit. Opportunities for such experiences abound at Blank High School. In collaboration with student leaders the offices of Student Life aim to enrich the lives of students through engagement in student organizations, service learning, student activities, recreation, and leadership development.

Facilities

Guilford University is a LAD Silver certified facility serving grades 9-12 on a 145-acre site. Highlights include two three-story academic wings, athletic fields, stadium, an activities wing with a three-station gymnasium; and a performing arts wing with 1,000-seat auditorium.
